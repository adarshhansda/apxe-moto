Frequently Asked

What's your return policy?

30-day no-questions-asked returns. Helmet must be unworn with all tags attached. Free return shipping within the US.

How do I find my size?

Measure your head circumference with a soft tape measure about 1 inch above your eyebrows. Check our size guide on the products page.

Do you ship internationally?

Yes! We ship to 40+ countries. International shipping rates calculated at checkout. Duties and taxes may apply.

What warranty do you offer?

All APEX helmets come with a 5-year manufacturer warranty against defects in materials and workmanship.
