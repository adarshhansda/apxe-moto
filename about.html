Our Story

About APEX MOTO

Founded by riders, for riders. We exist to push the boundaries of helmet safety and design — every single day.

Born on the Track, Built for the Street

APEX MOTO was founded in 2018 by aerospace engineer Marcus Koenig and MotoGP racer James Torres. They saw a critical gap in the market: no helmet combined true race-level protection with everyday riding comfort.

After 3 years of intensive R&D and over 2,000 controlled impact tests at our California facility, the X-1 Pro was born. It exceeded every safety standard we set — and redefined what riders could expect from a helmet.

Today, APEX helmets protect riders in 40+ countries worldwide, from MotoGP paddocks to daily commuters navigating city streets.
