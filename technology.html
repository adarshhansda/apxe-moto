Engineering

Technology That Leads

Every component is the result of years of R&D, wind-tunnel testing, and real-world validation. Nothing ships until it survives the impossible.

Impact Resistance

Tri-composite carbon shell absorbs and disperses impact energy across three matrix layers for maximum protection.

  • Multi-directional impact protection
  • Energy dispersion technology
  • Exceeds ECE 22.06 standards
  • SHARP 5-star rated

Aero Ventilation

12-channel airflow system maintains optimal temperature at any riding speed — tested in wind tunnels up to 200mph.

  • Front intake vents
  • Rear exhaust ports
  • Channeled EPS liner
  • Anti-fog visor system

Lightweight Build

Race-spec carbon fiber construction keeps weight under 1.25 kg without compromising structural integrity.

  • Premium carbon fiber shell
  • Optimized resin matrix
  • Strategic reinforcement zones
  • Weight: 1.24kg (size M)

EPS Impact Matrix

Variable-density expanded polystyrene foam liner tuned to absorb peak-force spikes across all impact scenarios.

  • Multi-density formulation
  • Optimized crush zones
  • Emergency release system
  • Removable/washable liner

Visor Technology

Optical-grade polycarbonate visor with Pinlock anti-fog insert and scratch-resistant hard coating.

  • Pinlock-ready visor
  • UV400 protection
  • Quick-release mechanism
  • Wide field of view

Noise Reduction

Aerodynamic shell design and neck roll seal minimize wind noise for a quieter, less fatiguing ride.

  • Wind tunnel optimized
  • Noise: 82dB at 100km/h
  • Neck roll seal
  • Chin curtain included
